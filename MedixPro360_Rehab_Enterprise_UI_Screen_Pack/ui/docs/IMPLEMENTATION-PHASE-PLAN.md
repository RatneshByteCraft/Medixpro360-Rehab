# Implementation Phase Plan

## UI-1: Shell and Workspaces

Effective-navigation contract, role workspace shell, My Day framework, global search, tasks, notifications, permission-aware actions and reusable loading/empty/error/denied states.

## UI-2: Platform and Tenant Administration

Super Admin platform operations, tenant administration, support access controls, audit and AI governance.

## UI-3: Client Registry, Intake and Admission

Client search/duplicate matching, guided intake, readiness, consent/documents, admission and contextual Client Workspace.

## UI-4: Clinical Assessment, Treatment Planning and MDT

Sectioned assessments, signing/versioning, plan lifecycle, MDT preparation/meeting/actions and client timeline.

## UI-5: Therapy, Medication and Standardized Assessments

Therapist My Day, session documentation with embedded AI, prescriber workflow, MAR, assessment queues and trends.

## UI-6: Nursing, Residential and Facility Operations

Shift workspaces, observations, medication due, handover, incidents, leave, facility/bed operations.

## UI-7: Home-Care Operations

Coordinator control centre, dispatch, caregiver coverage, visit execution/review and exceptions.

## UI-8: Discharge, Aftercare, Family and Portals

Discharge blockers, aftercare queues, family engagement and permission-scoped portals.

## UI-9: Billing, Inventory, Documents and Consent

Operational finance, stock ledger, contextual documents/consent and audit history.

## UI-10: Analytics, Privacy, Compliance and Audit

Actionable drill-down analytics, sensitive-record controls, retention, compliance and audit workspaces.

## UI-11: AI Embedding and Governance

Contextual AI panels, provenance/evidence, human review, feedback, evaluation, provider governance and cost/safety monitoring.

Each phase requires build, route review, role review, workflow review, accessibility review, traceability update and API integration status review before the next phase.
